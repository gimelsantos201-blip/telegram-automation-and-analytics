This week vs last week

Views were 24,386, down 17.8% WoW. Views per link were 406, down 5.4%.

OVERALLMost of the fall is capture loss: 13 of 73 links have no usable screenshot, against 3 last week. Viber lost 5 of 12, yet its views per link rose 6.6%.

Days. Friday (−39.6%) and Monday (−48.4%) fell mainly on lost Viber links: Viber −51% and −64%, other channels −8% and −12%. Viber rose 7–20% on the days it lost no link. Per post, Saturday opened strongest (909 day-one views).

Behaviour. Bio was swapped daily on 8–10 Sep, and its day-two pull vanished: Viber bio added 1,279–1,353 views on day two last week, 142–169 now. Over 48 hours, bio ranks last on Telegram and WhatsApp. Day-two pull moved to Viber broadcast: all three measured links peaked on day two.

Also. Page A’s top post was a CDKEY drop again (86% of the page). Page B’s +142% is one interactive post (69%). Rewards’ 14 Sep Night Owl ran on an off-scheme link and recorded zero.
